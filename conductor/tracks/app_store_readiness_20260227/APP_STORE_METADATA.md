## Description
Take control of your notes and knowledge with Synaps Notes, the privacy-first iOS app that brings powerful AI processing directly to your device. 

Key Features:
- **Private, Local AI Chat:** Chat with your notes and documents using a high-performance local LLM (`llama.cpp`). Your data never leaves your device.
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG):** Enhance AI responses with contextual information retrieved from your own knowledge base.
- **Smart Knowledge Ingestion:** Easily import documents and images. Synaps Notes uses Apple Vision OCR to extract text with precision.
- **Secure Cloud Sync:** Synchronize your knowledge base across your iOS devices using Google Drive with robust backup and recovery.
- **Built for iOS:** A native SwiftUI experience designed for performance, efficiency, and a seamless feel.

Experience the future of intelligent note-taking with absolute privacy.
