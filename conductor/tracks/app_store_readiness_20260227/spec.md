# Track: App Store Readiness (20260227)

## Track Overview
Prepare Synaps Notes for initial App Store submission. This involves an audit of existing assets (icons, launch screen), compliance checks for local AI and Google integration, and generation of reviewer-facing metadata.

## Core Goals
1. **Asset Audit:** Verify App Store Icon (1024x1024 opaque), app icons, and launch screen compliance.
2. **Reviewer Guidance:** Generate an MD file containing the exact text for the "App Review Information" page in App Store Connect.
3. **Compliance Check:** Verify `Info.plist` strings (privacy, description) and local AI inference (`llama.cpp`) for App Store compliance.
4. **Metadata Preparation:** Draft the App Store Description, Keywords, and What's New.

## Requirements
- **App Review Instructions:** Detailed walkthrough of the app's functionality for Apple's reviewers.
- **Privacy Policy URL:** (Placeholder or existing link check).
- **Test Account:** Provide clear credentials for Google Sign-In (if required for review).
- **Compliance Strings:** Ensure all required purpose strings in `Info.plist` are professional and accurate.

## Out of Scope
- Marketing campaigns or website development beyond the required support URL.
- Functional feature development (beyond compliance-related fixes).
