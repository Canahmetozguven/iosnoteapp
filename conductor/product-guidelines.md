# Synaps Notes (iOS) - Product Guidelines

## Prose Style
- **Clarity:** Use clear, concise, and professional language throughout the app.
- **Tone:** Helpful and empowering, focusing on user productivity and security.
- **Terminology:** Consistent use of terms (e.g., "Notes," "Knowledge Base," "Sync").

## Branding & Aesthetics
- **Native Experience:** Adhere to Apple's Human Interface Guidelines (HIG) for a seamless iOS feel.
- **Clean Design:** Prioritize whitespace and typography to ensure readability.
- **Symbolism:** Use SF Symbols for a consistent and recognizable iconography.
- **Modern Polishing:** Ensure interactive feedback and smooth transitions.

## UX Principles
- **Privacy First:** Clearly communicate local processing and ensure sensitive operations are transparent.
- **Performance:** Optimize local inference and background tasks to maintain a responsive UI.
- **Offline Capability:** Ensure core functionality remains available without internet connectivity.
- **Contextual AI:** Integrate AI features as natural extensions of the note-taking workflow, rather than isolated tools.
